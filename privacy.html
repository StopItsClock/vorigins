Privacy

The short version: this site keeps nothing about you.

No cookies, no tracking

vorigins.stream sets no cookies, runs no analytics and shows no adverts. It does not ask for or store anything about you. The only thing it remembers is that you have already seen the opening logo, for as long as the tab stays open, so it does not play again on every visit.

What your browser fetches

Like any web page, loading this one makes your browser contact the services that supply its parts. Each of them sees your IP address, as every website you visit does:

  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, which host the site and the live war map;
  • Google Fonts, for the lettering;
  • cdnjs, for the map library;
  • MCHeads, for some players' Minecraft heads.

Twitch

Whether a member is live on Twitch, and their stream's title, is public information that Twitch publishes for anyone. The site shows it only for members who have asked to be listed, and links to their channel. Nothing about you is sent to Twitch unless you follow one of those links.

Who runs this

This site is run by the V-Origins team. It is not an official Minecraft service and is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Twitch.

To ask about anything here, or to have your details removed from the members list, contact the team on the V-Origins Discord, or at contact@vorigins.stream.
